## hsk-words.js

A list of 10,896 words with their HSK levels, parsed from the text layer of the
published HSK syllabus (HSK 考试大纲, released November 2025, levels 1–6 and 7–9).
Each word keeps the lowest level it appears at. The word list and level
assignments come from that syllabus; check the publisher's terms before reusing
this file outside personal study or teaching preparation. HSK is a trademark of
its owner; this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the HSK publisher.
If you hold rights in this data and want it removed, open an issue.

The pinyin in this file is generated by pypinyin (see below), not copied from the
syllabus, and can be wrong for polyphonic words.

## pinyin-map.js

Single-character pinyin generated with [pypinyin](https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin)
0.54 (MIT License), `Style.TONE`, most common reading per character.
